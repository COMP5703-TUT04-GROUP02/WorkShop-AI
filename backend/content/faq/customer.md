## Account and profile

### CUST-ACC-001

**Question: How do I create a customer account?**

**Answer:** Open the sign-in screen and choose registration. Enter your name and email address, select Send code, and enter the six-digit code mailed to that address. Then create and confirm your password, accept the Terms of Use, select the Customer role, and create the account. The email address is verified as part of registration.

### CUST-ACC-002

**Question: How do I verify my email address or phone number?**

**Answer:** Email is verified with a six-digit code sent to your inbox. Registration verifies the email before creating the account; an existing unverified account can request a new code from the verification screen or Account and Security. If the code does not arrive, check the spam folder and use Resend after the countdown ends. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and a new code replaces the previous one. Phone verification is still a demo and accepts any six digits after a phone number has been added to the profile.

### CUST-ACC-003

**Question: How do I change my password?**

**Answer:** If your account uses email-and-password sign-in, open Account and Security, enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. The new password must contain at least eight characters. An account that only uses Google or Apple sign-in does not have a Workshop AI password to change.

### CUST-ACC-004

**Question: How do I change my email address?**

**Answer:** Open Account and Security, enter the new email address, and select Send code. Enter the six-digit code mailed to the new address to confirm the change. The new address becomes your verified sign-in email immediately after the code is accepted.

### CUST-ACC-005

**Question: How do I update my customer profile?**

**Answer:** Open Account, select Edit Profile, update the available personal and contact information, and save your changes.

### CUST-ACC-006

**Question: Can I sign in with Google or Apple?**

**Answer:** Yes, when the option is shown on the sign-in screen. Google sign-in is available on web, iOS, and Android. Sign in with Apple is available on iOS. If the social account is new to Workshop AI, choose the Customer role and accept the Terms of Use to finish creating the account.

## Finding and contacting providers

### CUST-DIS-001

**Question: How do I find a service provider?**

**Answer:** Use the search field on the customer home screen to search for a service or business. You can refine the results by location, distance, rating, price, service tag, availability, and sort order.

### CUST-DIS-002

**Question: What does “Open now” mean?**

**Answer:** “Open now” is based on the provider's configured working hours and any date-specific availability changes. It indicates current availability and does not guarantee that a particular appointment time will be accepted.

### CUST-DIS-003

**Question: What information can I check before choosing a provider?**

**Answer:** Open the provider's profile to review their services, service areas, business details, availability, portfolio, ratings, and customer reviews before starting a chat or requesting a booking.

### CUST-DIS-004

**Question: Why can’t I find a provider in search results?**

**Answer:** The provider may not match your search filters, service area, requested time, or price range. Only providers with approved qualifications are available to customers. Clear some filters or try a broader search.

### CUST-DIS-005

**Question: How do I save a provider for later?**

**Answer:** Open an approved provider's profile and add the provider to your favourites. You can view or remove saved providers from your favourites list.

### CUST-DIS-006

**Question: How do I contact a provider?**

**Answer:** Open the provider's profile and start a chat. You can use the chat to discuss your needs before or during a booking.

## Booking requests

### CUST-BKG-001

**Question: How do I request a booking?**

**Answer:** Open a provider's profile or an existing chat with the provider, select Request booking, complete the booking form, and select Send request. The provider is notified after the request is created.

### CUST-BKG-002

**Question: What information is required for a booking request?**

**Answer:** Enter a booking title, service description, service address, start time, and end time. The title must contain no more than 30 words, and the end time must be later than the start time.

### CUST-BKG-003

**Question: Can I attach photos or videos to a booking request?**

**Answer:** Yes. You can attach up to five images and two videos, with no more than seven files in total. Each image must be 2 MB or less, and each video must be 10 MB or less. Supported image formats are JPG, JPEG, and PNG; supported video formats are MP4 and MOV.

### CUST-BKG-004

**Question: Can I edit a booking request after sending it?**

**Answer:** You can edit the title, description, address, and requested time while the booking is still pending and before the provider responds. Once the provider has responded, the booking can no longer be edited through the pending-request form.

### CUST-BKG-005

**Question: What happens after I send a booking request?**

**Answer:** The booking starts in a pending state and the provider receives a notification. A chat room is created automatically if the booking was not created from an existing chat. The provider can then send a quote or reject the request.

### CUST-BKG-006

**Question: Where can I check my booking progress?**

**Answer:** Open Bookings and select the booking to view its current status, request details, price breakdown, payment status, attachments, refund information, and available actions. Booking updates may also appear in the related chat and Notifications.

## Quotes and payments

### CUST-PAY-001

**Question: What is included in the provider's first quote?**

**Answer:** The first quote shows the visit fee and the expected service amount. The visit fee is the amount due before the provider visits. The expected service amount is an estimate and may be replaced by a final quote after the provider arrives and assesses the work.

### CUST-PAY-002

**Question: What happens when I accept the first quote?**

**Answer:** Accepting the first quote creates a visit-fee payment. Complete the visit-fee checkout before the provider starts the visit.

### CUST-PAY-003

**Question: What happens if I decline the first quote?**

**Answer:** Enter a reason for declining the quote. The booking returns to a pending state, and the provider can review your reason and send a revised quote.

### CUST-PAY-004

**Question: Why are there two payment stages?**

**Answer:** The visit fee covers the provider's visit and is paid first. After arrival and assessment, the provider sends a final quote for the service itself. You review that quote before completing the service-fee payment.

### CUST-PAY-005

**Question: How do I pay for a booking?**

**Answer:** Select Pay or Continue payment from the booking when payment is due. The app opens a secure iyzico checkout page. Workshop AI is currently a demo, so payments are simulated and no real money is charged.

### CUST-PAY-006

**Question: What should I do if I close the checkout page before finishing?**

**Answer:** Return to the booking and select Continue payment if the existing checkout is still available. If the payment shows a failure, refresh the booking and use the available payment action to try again.

### CUST-PAY-007

**Question: What should I do if a payment fails or stays pending?**

**Answer:** Refresh the booking and review the payment status and payment note. If the app offers Pay or Continue payment, use that action to retry or resume checkout. Do not create repeated payments while a checkout is still processing.

### CUST-PAY-008

**Question: What is the final quote?**

**Answer:** The final quote is the provider's service price after arriving and assessing the work. It may differ from the expected service amount in the first quote. Review the final amount and any message from the provider before accepting it.

### CUST-PAY-009

**Question: What happens if I decline the final quote?**

**Answer:** Enter a reason for declining it. The booking returns to final-quote negotiation, and the provider can send a revised final quote. You can also close the booking without requesting a refund, but closing the booking does not return money automatically.

### CUST-PAY-010

**Question: What happens when I accept the final quote?**

**Answer:** Accepting the final quote creates the service-fee payment. Complete the displayed checkout before the service proceeds to completion.

### CUST-PAY-011

**Question: Where can I view my payment history?**

**Answer:** Open Account and select Payments to view payment records associated with your bookings, including their amount, payment stage, and current status.

## Visit and service completion

### CUST-SVC-001

**Question: What happens after I pay the visit fee?**

**Answer:** The provider can begin the visit process. After arriving, the provider records the arrival and prepares the final quote for the service work.

### CUST-SVC-002

**Question: What should I do if the provider does not arrive?**

**Answer:** Check the booking status and contact the provider through the related chat. Do not confirm completion. Use the actions available on the booking to cancel or request a refund when the booking and payment status allow it.

### CUST-SVC-003

**Question: How do I know when the provider has completed the service?**

**Answer:** The booking changes to Awaiting confirmation after the provider marks the work as complete and submits completion evidence. Open the booking to review its details before selecting Confirm complete.

### CUST-SVC-004

**Question: Will confirming completion charge me again?**

**Answer:** No. Confirming completion finalizes the booking and does not create an additional customer charge.

### CUST-SVC-005

**Question: What should I do if I am not satisfied with the completed service?**

**Answer:** Do not select Confirm complete until you have reviewed the work. Contact the provider through the booking chat and use Request refund if that action is available for the paid booking.

## Cancellations and refunds

### CUST-REF-001

**Question: Can I cancel a booking before the visit fee is paid?**

**Answer:** Yes. A booking can be cancelled while it is pending, quoted, awaiting payment, or waiting for an unpaid visit fee. Open the booking and select Cancel when the action is available.

### CUST-REF-002

**Question: Can I cancel after the visit fee has been paid?**

**Answer:** The booking may be closed after the visit-fee stage when the Cancel action is available. Closing or cancelling a booking does not automatically refund a paid fee. Submit a separate refund request if you want paid money to be reviewed for refund.

### CUST-REF-003

**Question: Does cancelling a booking automatically refund my payment?**

**Answer:** No. Cancellation closes the booking but does not automatically refund a paid visit fee or service fee. Refunds use a separate request and review process.

### CUST-REF-004

**Question: When can I request a refund?**

**Answer:** A refund can be requested after the provider starts the visit, when the booking has a paid payment and the Request refund action is available. Each booking can be refunded successfully only once, and you cannot start another request while an earlier request for the same payment is still open.

### CUST-REF-005

**Question: Can I request a partial refund?**

**Answer:** Yes. Enter a refund amount that is greater than zero and no more than the paid amount. Leave the refund amount blank to request a refund of the full paid amount. A reason is required for every refund request.

### CUST-REF-006

**Question: What happens after I request a refund?**

**Answer:** The booking changes to a refunding state and the provider reviews the request. The provider can approve it or reject it with a reason. The booking displays the current refund status.

### CUST-REF-007

**Question: What can I do if the provider rejects my refund request?**

**Answer:** Open the refunding booking, enter an appeal reason, and select Submit appeal. Only a refund request rejected by the provider can be appealed. The Workshop AI team then reviews the appeal.

### CUST-REF-008

**Question: Can I cancel a refund request?**

**Answer:** You can cancel the refund request while the Cancel refund action is available. Cancelling the request restores the booking to the status it had before the refund request was created.

### CUST-REF-009

**Question: How long does a refund take?**

**Answer:** Workshop AI does not currently promise a refund processing time. The booking is only marked as fully or partially refunded after the payment processor confirms the refund.

## Reviews

### CUST-REV-001

**Question: When can I review a provider?**

**Answer:** You can submit a review after the booking is completed. The review option is not available while the booking is still active, awaiting confirmation, cancelled, or refunding.

### CUST-REV-002

**Question: How many reviews can I submit for one booking?**

**Answer:** You can submit one review for each completed booking. Choose a rating and add a comment about your experience.

## AI assistant

### CUST-AI-001

**Question: What can the AI assistant help me with?**

**Answer:** The AI assistant can help find providers, check reviews and booking information, collect details for a booking draft, and prepare supported booking or payment-related actions.

### CUST-AI-002

**Question: Does an AI booking draft create a real booking immediately?**

**Answer:** No. The assistant may collect missing information over several messages and show a draft. A real booking is only created after all required details are present and you confirm the prepared action.

### CUST-AI-003

**Question: Why does the AI assistant ask me to confirm an action?**

**Answer:** Actions that change booking, payment, quote, completion, or refund data require confirmation. Review the confirmation card carefully before approving it. Searching and checking information do not require the same confirmation step.

### CUST-AI-004

**Question: What happens if an AI confirmation expires?**

**Answer:** AI confirmation actions expire after 30 minutes. If an action expires, ask the assistant to prepare it again and review the new confirmation before approving it.

### CUST-AI-005

**Question: Why is the AI assistant unavailable?**

**Answer:** The AI assistant may be temporarily unavailable. Other customer features, including provider search, chat, bookings, and payments, can still be used when AI is unavailable.
